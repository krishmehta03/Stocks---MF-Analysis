Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information WealthLenz ("we", "us") collects when you use wealthlenz.in (the "Service"), how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. By creating an account or using the Service, you agree to this policy.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Account information:
  • Your full name and email address.
  • Your password, if you sign up with email. It is stored only in hashed (scrambled) form by our authentication provider. We cannot see it.
  • If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address from Google, and Google may also share your profile picture. We never receive your Google password.
Portfolio information you enter or import:
  • Stock holdings: name, exchange, quantity, buy price and buy date.
  • Mutual fund holdings: fund name, units, buy NAV and purchase date.
  • Transactions and realised trades: type, name, quantity, price, dates and any notes you add.
Plan and payment information:
  • Your plan (Free or Pro), when it started and when it expires.
  • For Pro purchases: the Razorpay order and payment identifiers, the plan bought and the payment status.
  • We do not receive or store your card, UPI or bank details. Payments are handled by Razorpay on its own secure checkout.
Usage information:
  • Basic usage events such as logins, pages visited inside the app and features used, each with a date and time.
  • The time of your last login.
  • We run this tracking ourselves, in our own database. We do not use third-party analytics or advertising trackers.
Technical information:
  • Like most websites, our hosting and infrastructure providers automatically record technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, for security and reliability.

2. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

  • To create and secure your account and to provide portfolio tracking, valuations and analytics.
  • To send account emails, such as email verification and password reset.
  • To process Pro plan payments and apply your plan.
  • To generate the automated insights shown in the AI Advisor and portfolio alerts. These are produced by our own software from the holdings you have entered.
  • To understand which features are used so we can fix problems and improve the Service.
  • To protect the Service from abuse and to meet legal obligations.
  • We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information.

3. WHO WE SHARE INFORMATION WITH

We use trusted service providers to run the Service. They process data only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase: database and sign-in (stores your account and portfolio data).
  • Render: hosting for our application servers.
  • Razorpay: payment processing for Pro plans.
  • Resend: sending account emails such as verification and password reset.
  • Google: only if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • Content delivery and font providers (such as jsDelivr, cdnjs and Google Fonts) and the Razorpay checkout script: your browser loads code or fonts from them, so they receive standard connection data such as your IP address.
  • Market data sources (Yahoo Finance and MFAPI): we send them security or fund names to fetch prices and NAVs. We do not send your name, email or account details.
  • UptimeRobot: checks whether our website is online. It does not receive any user data.

We may also disclose information if required by law, court order or a lawful request from a government authority, or to protect the rights, safety or security of WealthLenz or its users. If WealthLenz is ever sold or merged, user information may transfer to the new owner, who must honour this policy.

Some of these providers operate servers outside India, so your information may be processed in other countries.

4. COOKIES AND BROWSER STORAGE

We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking cookies. We use your browser's local storage and session storage, which work like small cookies, only for essentials:

  • Keeping you signed in, and remembering your "Remember me" choice.
  • Remembering your theme and display preferences.
  • Temporarily blocking repeated failed sign-in attempts.
  • Holding your plan details so the app loads faster.

Because this storage is essential to the Service, we show a short notice rather than asking for consent. You can clear it at any time in your browser settings, but you will be signed out. If we ever add analytics or advertising tools that need consent, we will ask first.

5. DATA STORAGE AND SECURITY

  • Your data is stored in a Supabase (PostgreSQL) database, and each user can access only their own records through database-level access rules (Row Level Security).
  • Data travels between your browser and our servers over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Passwords are hashed and are never stored in plain text.
  • No system is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, and you are responsible for keeping your password confidential.
  • If we become aware of a data breach that affects you, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law.

6. HOW LONG WE KEEP INFORMATION

  • We keep your information while your account is active.
  • When you delete your account from Account Settings, we delete your holdings, transactions, subscription records, usage events, profile and sign-in record from our live systems. Copies in routine backups may remain for up to 30 days before they are overwritten.
  • Razorpay and other payment institutions keep their own records of payments as required by law and their own policies. We cannot delete those.
  • We may keep limited information where needed to meet legal obligations, resolve disputes or prevent fraud.

7. YOUR RIGHTS AND CHOICES

You can:

  • View and update your name in Account Settings.
  • Delete your account and your data yourself in Account Settings.
  • Ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct information that is wrong or incomplete.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time by deleting your account.

To make a request, email privacy@wealthlenz.in from the address registered to your account. We aim to reply within 30 days. We may need to confirm your identity first.

8. CHILDREN

The Service is intended for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. LINKS TO OTHER SITES

The Service may link to other websites, such as fund houses or government sites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

10. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the "Last updated" date above, and if the changes are significant we will tell you by email or a notice in the app. Continuing to use the Service after a change means you accept the updated policy.

11. CONTACT AND GRIEVANCES

For privacy questions, requests or complaints, email privacy@wealthlenz.in. For general support, email support@wealthlenz.in. We will acknowledge complaints promptly and aim to resolve them within 30 days.
