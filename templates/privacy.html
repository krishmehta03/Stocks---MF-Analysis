Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Account Information:
  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Password (encrypted, never stored in plain text)
Portfolio Data:
  • Stock holdings you add manually
  • Mutual fund holdings
  • Buy prices and quantities
  • Buy dates
Usage Data:
  • Pages visited
  • Features used
  • Login timestamps

2. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

  • To provide portfolio tracking and analytics services
  • To send account-related emails (verification, password reset)
  • To calculate portfolio performance
  • To generate AI-powered insights
  • We do NOT use your data for advertising
  • We do NOT sell your data to any third party

3. DATA STORAGE AND SECURITY

  • All data is stored securely on Supabase (PostgreSQL database)
  • Data is encrypted in transit using SSL/TLS
  • Passwords are hashed using industry-standard encryption
  • We use Row Level Security (RLS) to ensure each user can only access their own data

4. DATA SHARING

We share your data with:

  • Supabase (database hosting)
  • Razorpay (payment processing)

We do NOT share your data with:

  • Advertisers
  • Data brokers
  • Any other third parties

5. YOUR RIGHTS

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and all data
  • Export your portfolio data
  • Withdraw consent at any time

6. COOKIES

We use minimal cookies for:

  • Authentication (keeping you logged in)
  • Session management

We do not use advertising cookies.

7. DATA RETENTION

  • Active account data is retained while your account exists
  • Deleted accounts: data is permanently deleted within 30 days of account deletion

8. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

This service is not intended for users under 18 years of age.

9. CHANGES TO PRIVACY POLICY

We will notify users of significant changes via email.

10. CONTACT

For privacy concerns: privacy@wealthlenz.in
