Frequently asked questions

What is WealthLenz?

WealthLenz is a portfolio tracker for Indian investors. You add your stocks and mutual funds once and see live valuations, gains and losses, XIRR and CAGR, and sector and asset allocation in one dashboard.

Is WealthLenz free?

Yes. The Free plan covers up to 10 stocks and 3 mutual funds with no card required. The Pro plan costs Rs 199 per month, removes those limits and adds live alerts and the AI portfolio diagnostics.

Does WealthLenz give investment advice?

No. WealthLenz is a tracking and analytics tool. It shows data about your own holdings and does not recommend that you buy or sell any security. Please read our Disclaimer for details.

Is my portfolio data private?

Yes. Your holdings are stored in a database with row-level security, so only your account can read them. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising. See our Privacy Policy for details.
